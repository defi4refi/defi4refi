# Apply for a free build

defi4refi is a public-goods development studio. We design and ship
open-source protocol codebases — token, staking, bonds, treasury, governance,
dApp frontends — **free** to selected ReFi / public-goods teams, funded by
donations to the studio (donors receive no services in return).

## How to apply

1. **Open an issue** using the
   [apply-for-build template](../../issues/new?template=apply-for-build.md).
2. **Or talk to us first** — join the community at
   [t.me/defi4refi](https://t.me/defi4refi).

## What happens next

- We score your application against the published rubric (ReFi alignment and
  open-source commitment are hard gates).
- Accepted projects sign a lightweight open-source contribution agreement:
  fixed scope, OSI license, no warranty, client deploys mainnet.
- We scope a one-page PRD, then an agent-assisted build runs inside *your*
  repo — you own the code from day one.
- Handoff includes docs, a runbook, and an audit-readiness statement.
  We mint a hypercert and publish a short writeup per engagement.

## Capacity

Max 2 concurrent protocol builds + 1 design sprint. Beyond that, a published
waitlist — first-come by rubric score, not by who can pay.
